# MK Receipt Pro 1.1.0-dev.13 — Separate VAT and Income Tax reporting tracks

- Reporting center clearly separates VAT exempt-dealer declaration from annual income-tax reporting.
- VAT section shows annual turnover based on active receipts recorded in the system.
- Income-tax section shows business income, recorded expenses, accounting difference, and missing expense attachments.
- Annual export package creates separate VAT and Income Tax folders.
- No automatic decision is made about tax-deductible expenses.
- Official filing remains in the Israel Tax Authority systems.
